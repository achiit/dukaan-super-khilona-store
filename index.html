Our story

It started with a small corner of my uncle's shop

In 2009, my uncle, Shri Ramesh Granth, gave me one glass counter at the front of his stationery shop on Station Road. I filled it with wooden blocks, a few teddy bears and some wind-up cars. I remember a boy standing there for twenty minutes, deciding between a red car and a blue car. His mother finally bought both.

That was the day I understood that a toy is never a small purchase for a parent. They want it to be safe, to last, and to make a child smile. By 2014, the toys had outgrown the counter, and the whole shop became Super Khilona Store.

I still test new toys at the counter myself, because I will not sell anything I would not give to my own niece.

  • Every toy checked for sharp edges and loose parts
  • Honest age advice, we will tell you if a toy is too young or old for the child
  • Battery and remote toys tested at the counter before you leave
  • 7 day exchange on unused toys in original packing
  • Fair prices, no festival markups on regular toys
